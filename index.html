About TMISB

Techno Mission International School, Bhagalpur (TMISB) stands as a beacon of educational excellence. We believe in providing a holistic environment where academic rigor meets modern technology, ensuring our students are prepared for the challenges of tomorrow.

Our educational philosophy combines academic learning with critical thinking, creativity, and discipline. Rooted in Indian values and a global outlook, TMISB is committed to nurturing the unique potential within every child.

Frequently Asked Questions

The admission procedure follows a streamlined 5-step roadmap: (1) Admission Enquiry online or on-campus, (2) Registration with basic applicant details, (3) Interaction or Assessment based on age and grade readiness, (4) Document Verification of academic records and birth proof, and (5) Admission Confirmation upon fee payment and seat allotment. Visit our Admissions Page or call +91 9431214985 to begin.
For the 2026–27 academic year, age cut-offs are calculated as of 31 March 2026. Minimum entry age for Nursery is 3+ years, LKG is 4+ years, UKG is 5+ years, and Class I is 6+ years. Class-wise age bands progress accordingly through Primary, Middle, Secondary, and Senior Secondary wings.
Required documents include: (1) Official Birth Certificate, (2) Previous academic session report card or marksheet, (3) Transfer Certificate (TC) / School Leaving Certificate from the previous recognised school (Class II onwards), (4) Student & Parent/Guardian Aadhaar or ID proofs, and (5) Recent passport-size photographs of the student and parents.
TMISB is affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE Affiliation No. 330331), New Delhi, up to Senior Secondary (+2 Science & Commerce). In addition to holistic board curricula, we provide integrated classroom coaching and mentoring for national competitive examinations such as IIT-JEE, NEET, CA Foundation, and Olympiads.
Yes. TMISB maintains a dedicated fleet of GPS-monitored school buses covering all major arterial and suburban routes in Bhagalpur. Every bus is equipped with speed governors, first-aid kits, and fire safety equipment, operated by certified drivers, verified conductors, and female attendants for maximum student security.
